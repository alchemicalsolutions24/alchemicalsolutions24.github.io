Your Privacy Choices

9 to Zen shares device identifiers (IDFA/GAID) and coarse, IP-derived location data with our third-party advertising partners to deliver personalized ads. Under California law (CCPA/CPRA), this qualifies as a "sale" or "share" of personal information, even though we never sell your data for cash.

You have the right to opt out of this at any time, request a copy of the data we hold, or request deletion of it — and we will never restrict your ability to play 9 to Zen if you exercise any of these rights.

If you already have the app installed

Open 9 to Zen → tap the gear icon (Settings) → Legal & Privacy → Privacy Choices. This opens your ad-personalization consent choice directly and takes effect immediately — no account or email required.

To opt out without the app, or to request access/deletion

Email contact@alchemical-solutions.com with the right you'd like to exercise (opt-out, access, or deletion).

Since 9 to Zen accounts are anonymous by default, an access or deletion request needs your unique Zen Profile ID (visible in-app under Profile) or a screenshot of your settings screen, so we can locate and verify the correct record. A plain opt-out request doesn't require this.

We will act on opt-out requests within 15 business days, and on access/deletion requests within 45 days, as required by the CCPA/CPRA.
