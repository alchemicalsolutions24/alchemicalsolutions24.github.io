Privacy Center & Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026 | Effective Date: July 31, 2026
Alchemical Solutions ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of users who download, install, or interact with 9 to Zen and our associated online services (collectively, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use 9 to Zen, participate in Free Trials, or purchase Ascend Level access.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Credentials: When you register an account in 9 to Zen, we may collect your username, email address, and authentication preferences.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: If you contact us directly for technical assistance or legal inquiries, we collect your name, email address (contact@alchemical-solutions.com), and any information included in your message.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Gameplay and Progress Analytics: To deliver and optimize 9 to Zen, we collect usage metrics, puzzle progress, score milestones, and Ascend Level trial status.
  • Device and Diagnostic Data: We automatically gather technical details about your mobile device, including unique device identifiers, operating system version, app build version, performance logs, and crash reports.

1.3 Financial and Payment Information Disclosures

  • Free Trial Data Handling: We do not collect, request, or store credit card, debit card, or financial billing information when you activate or participate in a Free Trial for 9 to Zen or Ascend Level features.
  • In-App Purchase Transactions: Financial payment processing for purchases initiated via "Prompt to Buy" overlays is handled exclusively by third-party app marketplaces (such as the Apple App Store or Google Play Store). Alchemical Solutions never receives or stores your full payment card details on its servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Providing, maintaining, and restoring access to 9 to Zen features, including base tiers and Ascend Level capabilities.
  • Trial Management: Tracking the start, duration, and expiration of Free Trials without collecting financial credentials upfront.
  • Account State Preservation: Retaining your game progress and Ascend Level settings following trial expiration so you can resume seamlessly if you opt to purchase later.
  • App Improvement & Bug Fixing: Analyzing crash logs and performance analytics to improve puzzle mechanics and stability.
  • Communication: Responding to inquiries sent to contact@alchemical-solutions.com and sending essential transactional notifications (such as trial expiration prompts or system updates).

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Platform Partners & Analytics: We may utilize trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud backend services, analytics providers, and store distribution platforms) solely to support app infrastructure and performance analytics.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights, property, or safety.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of company assets, your information may be transferred as part of the business assets.

4. Data Retention and Expired Trial Policy

  • Active Accounts: We retain your account data and gameplay progress for as long as your account remains active.
  • Expired Free Trials: If your Free Trial expires without converting to a paid purchase, we retain your non-essential trial progress and Ascend Level metrics for ninety (90) days. This allows your progress to be restored if you choose to purchase access at a later date. Following this retention period, inactive trial data may be archived, anonymized, or deleted.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may request complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us at contact@alchemical-solutions.com.

5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including rights under California CCPA/CPRA, European GDPR, and state privacy laws), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request that we rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion: Request the permanent deletion of your account data.
  • Opt-Out Options: Manage in-app data collection choices through the settings menu within 9 to Zen.

To exercise any of these privacy rights, please submit a request to contact@alchemical-solutions.com.

6. Children's Privacy

9 to Zen is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data without proper authorization, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

7. Security Measures

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse. However, no data transmission over the internet or wireless network can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our legal requirements or software features. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy or via in-app notifications.
